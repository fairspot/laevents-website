What we do

We plan, build and run the whole market, from the stall map to the program on stage.

Market stalls

Hundreds of traders, from seasoned market sellers to locals clearing out their attic. We sell and place the stalls and look after the traders during the market.

Music and performances

Live music in the big market tent and performances throughout the weekend give our markets the atmosphere of a festival.

Tivoli and family fun

A travelling tivoli, activities for children and the classic banko evening make a day at the market fun for the whole family.

Our markets

Week 30 · Dyrskuepladsen, Horsens

Horsens Marked

One of the largest markets in Denmark, held for more than 30 years. Around 300 stalls, a travelling tivoli, live music and about 30,000 visitors every year. Part of the income supports local sports clubs in Horsens Municipality.

Visit horsens-marked.dk

Week 25 · Salten In partnership

Salten Marked

A market with roots in the early 1800s, organised together with Salten Borger og Grundejerforening and Herning Tivolipark. More than 200 stalls and 25,000–30,000 visitors every year.

Our product In development · launching 2027

FairSpot: stall booking for markets and fairs

Running our own markets showed us how much work goes into stall bookings: spreadsheets, emails, bank transfers and maps drawn by hand. So we are building FairSpot, a booking app for market organisers and stall holders.

FairSpot is developed and owned by LA Events. It will be available in English and Danish.

  1. Draw the market map. Organisers lay out the market as sections of stalls and set a price per metre.
  2. Book without an account. Stall holders find a market, choose metres and stall type, and pay a deposit online.
  3. Place and confirm. Organisers place the stalls on the map, give out spot numbers, take the balance and email every stall holder.
