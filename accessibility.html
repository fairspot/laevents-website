Accessibility statement

LA Events wants everyone to be able to use laevents.dk. This statement covers this website only. The websites of the individual markets are run separately.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have done

  • The site uses semantic HTML with headings, landmarks and a link to skip to the content.
  • Text and buttons meet the WCAG AA contrast requirements.
  • Everything can be used with a keyboard, and the keyboard focus is always visible.
  • Images have text alternatives.
  • The site works when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.
  • The site is available in English and Danish, and the page language is set for screen readers.
  • Smooth scrolling is turned off if your device asks for reduced motion.

How we checked

We have reviewed the site ourselves. It has not been assessed by an external party.

Tell us about problems

If something on the site is hard to use, email ulrich@laevents.dk and we will get back to you.
